## Findings

### Duplicated per unit: three tools are copied into every unit's records

1. **Mutation driver.** The M0–M1 review recommended one general driver. None was written, and every unit since has copied its predecessor's. The five M3 drivers (`0019-antiproducts`, `0019-linear`, `0020-duals`, `0020-rounding`, `0020-rounding-wu11`) share the same docstring and the same five functions (`mutate`, `timed`, `failed_builds`, `run`, `main`); they differ in the mutant table, the header path, the targets, and the label regex. `0020-rounding/mutate.py` and `0020-rounding-wu11/mutate.py` differ in 142 lines of `diff` output, almost all in the table. `0020-rounding/cases.py` and `0020-rounding-wu11/cases.py` are byte-identical. The reviewers wrote four more drivers of the same shape in the scratchpad, which are lost when it is cleared. Lessons 0060 and 0061 (anchors that occur exactly once; the build's exit before the tests') are re-implemented in each copy.
   - Recommendation: a unit with a plan and a review writes `tools/mutate` (or a snippet first, if the owner prefers to try it before promoting it) that takes a mutant table as data (file, anchor, replacement, occurrences), the targets, the label regex, and the preset, and keeps the record format of the M3 drivers (`out/<preset>.tsv`, selection `mutant:<name>`). Each unit's record then holds only its table and its output. The existing drivers stay where their records cite them. Present user: WU13a and WU13b (plan 0021 section 6 lists about 30 mutants), and every reviewer who runs its own mutants.
2. **Pinned before-and-after compile cost.** `performance/antiproducts-cost/measure.py`, `linear-cost/measure.py`, and `duals-cost/measure.py` repeat the same `extract`, `read_last_row`, and `compile_pinned` functions and the same docstring (git archive of the before revision, `taskset -c <cpu> tools/timed measure`, interleaved repeats); two also repeat `oriented` and `growth_unit` for the n = 4 to 16 growth units. W10 and P1 ask for this measurement in every core unit.
   - Recommendation: the same kind of unit makes one harness (a `tools/measure` subcommand, or a snippet) that takes the before revision, the units, the repeats, and the CPU. Present user: WU13a (plan 0021 section 2, W10 and P2 records).
3. **Diagnostics sizes.** `mutations/0019-antiproducts/diagnostics.py`, `mutations/0019-linear/diagnostics.py`, and `diagnostics/wu11a/diagnostics.py` each compile a list of compile-fail sources with `-fsyntax-only` on both compilers and print the first-line length, longest line, line count, and error count. They differ in the source list.
   - Recommendation: fold into the same harness as item 2, or index one of them here as the snippet to copy, with a source-list argument. Present user: WU13a (`records/diagnostics/wu13a-messages.md`).
