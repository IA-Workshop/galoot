## Findings

No blocking or major findings.

1. **Minor: plan 0023's outcome is stale.** At `docs/agent/plans/0023-wu16-rga3-model.md:253-254, 264, 271, 273`, departure 6 and "Not validated" still say that the objects' layout mutants never ran against the real header, though `05c1fa9` ran 20 of them and all 20 were killed. Departure 7 and "Open" still say that lint fails on `objects_test.cpp` and `point_groups_test.cpp`, though `bf630e7` and `613cab0` fixed it. The `records/rotation-tolerance` merge (`038c81f`) is listed as open. The W9 list leaves out `bf630e7`, `613cab0` and `05c1fa9`. A reader of the outcome would take resolved gaps to be open. Fix this before the merge; it does not block.
2. **Minor, plausible: the meet signs in `rigid_motions.py` are matched, not derived.** `line_meets_plane`, `planes_meet` and `three_planes_meet` (`test/reference/rigid_motions.py:294-345`) fix their signs by choice. `planes_meet` checks itself only up to scale. So the linear-algebra route confirms the meets independently only up to sign. Their signs rest on agreement with the geometric route. A sign error shared by both routes would pass, though the incidence zeros would not be affected. The plane's orientation is the exception: `self_check` pins it against CONVENTIONS 5.3. Recommendation: say this in the docstring or in note 0023.
3. **Info.** The head comment of `include/galoot/rga3/objects.hpp:20-24` says that `transform` is found by argument-dependent lookup. It omits the caveat about `using namespace std::ranges` that `transform.hpp` and the README give.
4. **Info.** C13 holds by inspection only. The include scan checks includes, not the use of `core::detail`.
