## Checks and how

### Mathematics, derived independently

- Recursion. e_A = e_a ∧ e_A' = e_a e_A' − e_a ⌋ e_A' for the lowest generator e_a, from e_i x = e_i ⌋ x + e_i ∧ x; the left contraction of a wedge blade by a vector is Σ_p (−1)^(p−1) g(e_i, e_bp) e_(B∖b_p) (an antiderivation); e_i ∧ e_B = (−1)^#{b in B, b < i} e_(B∪i), which is `reorder_sign(generator, blade)` (checked against `bits.hpp`: it counts generators of B below i). The code's contraction sign starts at +1 and the correction's at −1 and each flips on every set bit, whether or not the Gram entry is zero. Non-diagonal entries are read as g[i][j] and g[low][j]; null and degenerate generators enter as zero entries, skipped; shared generators need no special case. Correct.
- Capacity 2^(|A∪B|−1), or 1 for two scalars: every term that any call adds to any list lies on a subset of A ∪ B (each step removes a generator or adds one of A), with grade of the parity of |A| + |B| (the wedge and contraction terms change the grade of e_A' e_B by one; the corrections are e_(A'∖j) e_B, two below |A|); a list holds distinct blades, so every partial sum, and every `rest_product` (on A' ∪ B ⊆ A ∪ B), fits. At |A∪B| = 32 the shift is 31, defined. The bound is attained (e1 e2 in [[0,1],[1,0]]). The derivation in the `///` comment and in decision 0100 item 5 is right.
- Guards. `add_checked`: for −max ≤ x, y ≤ max, `max − y` (y > 0) and `−max − y` (y ≤ 0) cannot overflow and the test is exact. `multiply_checked`: |x| |y| ≤ max iff |y| ≤ ⌊max/|x|⌋; magnitudes are defined because the range excludes the minimum. Both bounds come from `numeric_limits`; no literal. The symmetric range rejects a product equal to −2^63, as decision 0101 says.
- Overflow input. (k − 1)I + J has eigenvalues k − 1 (four times) and k + 4, so det g = (k − 1)^4 (k + 4) ≈ 3.8 × 10^22 ≈ 2^75; `max / (k−1)^4 < k + 4` is the right integer form of "the product exceeds max", and (k − 1)^4 ≈ 1.15 × 10^18 fits. If no guard fired every step would be exact and the stored result would be det g > max, a contradiction, so a guard must fire. Sound. The rank-one objection to the plan's all-k form (decision 0100 item 11) is right.
- Hand values (A6), each re-derived: conformal(3) e4 e5 = −1 + e45, e5 e4 = −1 − e45, e4 e45 = e4, e45 e4 = −e4, e5 e45 = −e5, e45 e5 = e5, e45 e45 = 1; Witt e1 e2 = 1 + e12, e2 e1 = 1 − e12, e12 e12 = 1, e12 e1 = e1, e1 e12 = −e1; degenerate e123 e123 = 0; ℍ: e2 e12 = e1, e12 e1 = e2, e12 e12 = −1; M₂(ℝ) e12 e12 = 1; Pauli −1, −1; Dirac e1234² = (+1)(det = −1) = −1; R(3,0,0) e13 e23 = −e12. All agree with the test.
- Golden spot checks by hand in random form 1 (g = [[2,0,−3],[0,1,−3],[−3,−3,−2]]): e13 e3 = −2 e1 + 3 e3 and e12 e23 = −3 + 3 e12 + e13, both as written in `golden.hpp`; det g = −31, so e123² = (−1)^3 (−31) = 31.
- Research note 0013 (pseudoscalar square), checked step by step: diagonalize over ℚ with invertible T (T G Tᵀ = D); the wedge of the f_i is det T · I because the exterior product is alternating and metric-free; orthogonal vectors' product equals their wedge (induction, the contraction vanishes); reversal of n anticommuting factors gives (−1)^(n(n−1)/2) and the collapse gives Π d_i = det D = (det T)² det G; divide by (det T)² ≠ 0. **The derivation is correct**, for every symmetric form over a field of characteristic not 2, degenerate included. Two wording points for the note (F9). The table of six values agrees with my own.

## Findings

**Blocking (small)**

- F1. `blade_product.hpp:284`: the range check on the second operand is untested. Every beyond-n input puts the stray bit in `a` (`blade_product<4>(R<3,0,0>(), 0b1000, 0)` in both the compile-fail and the abort test), so the mutant that checks `a >> N` alone survives on both compilers in every mode. Under it, `blade_product<4>(R<3,0,0>(), 0, 0b1000)` returns {e4: 1} in a three-generator algebra with no diagnostic. The code is right; the test does not pin it. Add the stray bit in `b` to the compile-fail and abort tests (for example `beyond_n_second`), in a test-only commit.
- F2. `blade_product.hpp:48-54`, the `///` of the public `coefficient_type`: "a coefficient that would leave that range fails GALOOT_ASSERT where it is formed, so no coefficient wraps silently." At run time with `GALOOT_CHECKING` 0, which is the default for a user who only adds the headers, `GALOOT_ASSERT` does not evaluate its condition and an overflow is undefined behavior. Decision 0101 says this correctly; the interface comment does not (C8). Say "in a constant evaluation, and at run time in a checking build".

**Non-blocking**

- F3. The guarantee that `Capacity >= product_capacity(a, b)` suffices for every partial sum, on which WU6b's `plan_capacity` scratch depends, is pinned only by the widest-algebra `static_assert` (capacity 1) and by e1 e2 at capacity 2; every exhaustive test uses capacity 32. My excursion mutant (a temporary term on a blade outside the parity class, added and removed in the base case, so that every result is unchanged) survives every run-time test. Cheap fix: run every pair of the exhaustive list through `blade_product<K>` with K = `product_capacity(a, b)`, dispatched over the six powers of two 1 to 32.
- F4. No test at N = mask_bits − 1: with the range check skipped at N = 31, a mask with bit 31 is accepted silently in R(31,0,0), and nothing fails. Add a beyond-n case at N = 31 with the top bit.
- F5. Three guarded multiplications cannot fail: `multiply_checked(reorder_sign(...), c)` (±1 times a value already within ±max) and `multiply_checked(sign, entry)` at both sites (±1 times an `int`). Their removal is an equivalent mutant. Decision 0101 lists them as guarded sites ("the wedge sign, each Gram entry times its contraction sign"). They cost constant-evaluation steps on Clang, some share of the +11% (not measured). Remove them or say in 0101 that they cannot fire; the owner's or author's call (C12, code-style: guards that cannot fire).
- F6. Two reachable-looking guards have no killing test: `x.terms[k].coefficient * scale` and `(sign · entry) · scale`. An exact-integer port of the recursion that records the first operation to leave ±max (`reviews/2026-11-01-wu6a-blade-product/first_overflow.py`, seeds 1 to 3, 52 dense forms at n = 5 and 6 with entries up to 2^15 − 1, every pair, and the overflow test's form with both signs of the off-diagonal) found the first overflow always at the contraction product (1,388 pairs) or at a sum (100), never at these two sites. A killing input may not exist at n ≤ 6; at larger n the scale's degree grows with the depth of corrections, so I cannot call them equivalent. Record them in the mutation record as unresolved survivors; no change is needed.
- F7. Decision 0101, "At the compilers' default limits": "the dense table ... fails Clang's step limit at -O0 and -O2, and it fails there without the guard too". The unguarded run exists only at -O0 (`overflow-guard-default-limits:clang++:random3:-O0:unguarded`), the rows record exit 1 but not that the step limit was the cause, and these default-limit compiles are not produced by the committed `measure_guard.py`, which raises the limits; under V10 they are not reproducible from committed steps. Narrow the sentence to -O0 for the unguarded case, and commit the command.
- F8. Decision 0101's "the guard costs nothing measurable on GCC" is right as measured but its cause is not stated; GCC's constexpr call cache is the likely one (inferred: `multiply_checked` and `add_checked` are pure functions of constant arguments). Worth one clause, so that a later reading does not take the GCC figure as the guard's intrinsic cost.
- F9. Research note 0013: the remark "every coefficient of a product of wedge monomials is one" is garbled (it means an integer polynomial in the entries of G, note 0009 B3), and the integrality remark is not needed, since the derivation holds over ℚ and the identity is polynomial in G. The "Checked by" line should name this review. The merging agent or author edits the note; I did not.

At run time with Capacity 2^15 (full masks at n = 16) that is 512 KiB a level and up to 16 levels, about 8 MiB, the usual default stack. Decision 0100 item 9 notes the cost as inferred. No present test or use reaches it (the run-time tests use capacity 32); WU6b and the identity suite at n > 6 should keep run-time capacities small.
