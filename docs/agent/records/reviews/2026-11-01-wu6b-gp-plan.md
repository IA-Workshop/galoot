## Checks and how

### Correctness of the plan, derived independently

- `independent/` (in this record's directory): `cases.py` lists seven cases, `expected.py` computes each plan from `test/reference/clifford_reference.py` (`Algebra.mul`, which diagonalizes the form over fractions and changes basis by minors, a route that shares no code or method with the header's recursion), with each oriented blade's sign from the parity of its written index list, each pair's terms sorted by (grade, index list) and multiplied by s_a s_b, zeros dropped. `gen_cpp.py` writes a C++ unit that prints `gp_plan` for the same sets; for the rigid(3) cases it writes the sets as `blades<4, "e41 e42 ...">`, so the library's own name parsing is checked against the reference's parity. The cases: conformal(3) with mixed signs and sets of sizes 6 and 4 (including e12345); the degenerate form [[0,1,0],[1,0,0],[0,0,0]], `full<3>` with signs against a set that repeats e12 with both signs; rigid(3) line × motor and plane × point by name; the dense random form 3 (n = 5) with signs, sizes 5 × 4; the Witt two-mode form, vectors × bivectors including e21; an empty first set. Result: 210 terms, `diff` empty on GCC 16.2.1 and Clang 23.1.4 (`expected.txt` committed). The empty set gives no terms.
- Every hand value in `plan_test.cpp` re-derived: point × point (15 terms, e_j e_i = −e_ij, e4 e4 = 0); the ten sparse terms (e1n e1 = −e_n, e1n e2 = −e12n, e_n e_n and e1n e_n vanish); the repeated blade in R(3,0,0) (−e1 e1 = −1, e2 e1 = −e12); R(32,0,0) with −e32 (e1 (−e32) = −e1∧e32, (−e32) e1 = +e1∧e32). The test's diagonal rule counts inversions (i in A, j in B, i > j) and multiplies the common squares, which is the single-term rule. All agree.
- A set built for fewer generators in a larger algebra: `gp_plan<rigid(3), full<3>, full<3>>` equals the R(3,0,0) plan (`static_assert`, both compilers). The antiscalar squared as a one-blade plan builds at n = 12 and 16 on both (0.21 s, 0.15 s).
- Sign folding, positions, and order in `generate_gp` read against the definition: coefficient s_a s_b c, `a` and `b` the loop indices, terms in the product's canonical order, `out` the product's canonical mask. Correct. `plan_capacity` is the maximum of `product_capacity` over the pairs, starting at 1; with the WU6a derivation that a pair's capacity suffices for every partial list, the capacity is right, and my mutants show a capacity below it fails loudly, never silently (F1).

### Guards

- Before generation: the range check (both sets, every blade, `in_algebra` with n = `generator_count`, which equals N for every `metric<N>`), the sign check (`signs_valid` of both sets), and generation only under `if constexpr` of both. Each is killed by the compile-fail tests in my mutants: `||` in place of `&&` in each check and in the guard, the range check at n + 1, skipping the last blade, n set to `mask_bits`, the guard on range only or signs only. One note: the guard on range alone (`r_guard_range_only`) is killed only by `bad_sign` (sign 0), and only because the zero-coefficient check of the generated plan then fires; with `bad_sign_second`'s sign 2 nothing would. It is killed as the tests stand.
- After generation: five flags from one pass. Each is fired by a generator mutant (the author's record, rerun) and each bound is tested on hand-written plans (`defects_of` test). My mutants of `defects_of` (skip the first term, skip the last, numeric in place of canonical order, a new `a` requiring b not to fall, the set sizes swapped) are killed; `defects_of` called with n = `mask_bits` survives and is equivalent on a correct generator, as the author's five check removals are.
- `generate_gp` does not check signs. Its callers are `filled_gp` and `make_gp_plan`, both after the checks, and the tests; its `///` comment says it does not check them. With a sign of ±1 the folded product cannot overflow. No path reaches it unchecked outside the tests. The comments and decision 0102 items 3 and 4 are accurate on this point.

### Limits

- `limits.py`, `out/limits.tsv`: at the boundary points above, in Galoot's flags at -O0 and -O2, bare -O0, and bare -O2 without `_GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS`, every result agrees with the `///` comment: Clang builds n = 5 and stops at n = 6 (dense: 4, stops at 5) with "constexpr evaluation hit maximum step limit"; GCC builds n = 7 and stops at n = 8 (dense: 5, stops at 6) with "operation count exceeds limit of 33554432".
