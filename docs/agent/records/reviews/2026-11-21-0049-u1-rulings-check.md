## 2 Findings, most severe first

### Finding 1 (important, confirmed): "in any nondegenerate algebra" overstates Theorem B

`docs/agent/records/docs/misconceptions.md:94`, the card's row in section 5.2, column "Must say": the conventions chapter must say "that in any nondegenerate algebra the sandwich is `transform(x, bulk_dual(V))`", citing note 0029 section 2.4. Theorem B there is stated for "a nondegenerate diagonal form with squares ±1", and its proof uses G⁻¹ = G.

Failure: `core::from_gram` builds nondegenerate forms with other squares. In diag(2, 1, 1), with r = (e1 + e2) e1 and x = (2, −3, 5), the library gives r x r̃ = (16, 10, 30) and `transform(x, bulk_dual(r))` = (40, 28, 60), which are not proportional (`bulk_dual_print.cpp` and `bulk_dual_print.out`). It fails the same way in diag(2, 1), diag(3, −1), diag(1, 1, 2) and diag(−2, 1, 1), on 60 versors each (`bulk_dual_forms.py`), and for one mirror in diag(2, 1, 1) (`bulk_dual_probe.cpp`). An author who writes the sentence as specified publishes a false statement on the page meant for researchers. The statement does hold in conformal(3) for one even and two odd versors (`bulk_dual_probe.cpp`), which is not diagonal, so the true scope is wider than the note proves. Neither I nor the note has stated or proved that wider scope.

Fix: restrict the sentence to what is proved: "in R(p, q, 0) for any p and q", or "in a diagonal form whose squares are 1 or −1". The conformal case can be added only with a proof. Does not block the merge of the records. It must be fixed before U4 writes the section.

### Finding 2 (important, confirmed): an overload set with two sentences cannot pass check 3

Rule G2 (`authoring-rules.md:197`) lets one entry document "one public declaration, or one overload set". G4 gives one Signature block per overload. G3 (`:198`) gives one One sentence field, which must be "the declaration's `///` sentence" word for word. Check 3 of decision 0258 compares each declaration's sentence with its entry's.

Failure: `truncate` has two overloads that are not rejecting ones. `include/galoot/core/mv.hpp:1047` opens with its own sentence, and `:1064` opens with "truncate<Target::layout>(x) for a multivector type Target of x's algebra and scalar type, with any cv-qualification and reference." An entry for the set holds one sentence, so check 3 fails for the other overload. Nothing in G2 or G3 tells the author whether to split the entry, give the field one sentence per overload, or ask plan 0048 for one shared sentence.

Fix: add the rule to G3, for example "an entry for an overload set has one One sentence paragraph per overload whose sentence differs, each beside its Signature block". Or require one entry per declaration that carries its own sentence. Does not block.

### Finding 3 (important, confirmed): G2 forbids the macros page and member entries, beyond check 2

G2 says "An author writes no entry for a name the manifest lacks" and "A member or a rejecting overload has no entry of its own". Plan 0047 section 5 leaves the public macros out of the manifest, since plan 0044's `core.public_macros` pins them. Plan 0049 section 4 puts a macros page in the reference. Check 2 of decision 0258 asks that "each reference entry names a declaration that exists", and that a member or rejecting overload be covered by its owner's entry under a stated rule. It does not forbid a member its own entry, and it does not restrict entries to the manifest.

Failure: the author of the reference's macros page (U9) cannot write an entry for `GALOOT_CHECKING` or `GALOOT_ASSERT` without breaking G2. A member that deserves its own entry is also barred, although check 2 allows one.

Fix: in G2, write "no entry for a name that no header declares". Name the macros and their source of truth (`core.public_macros`) as the second list that check 2 reads. Say that a member or rejecting overload *may* go under its owner's Covers rather than *must*. Does not block.

### Finding 4 (minor, confirmed): G3's word-for-word sentence meets rule W6 and has no code spans to keep

G3 says the One sentence keeps "the same words, signs, and code spans" as the header. The headers' `///` comments mostly have no code spans: `product.hpp` has no backtick at all, and `transform.hpp` has four. They name calls unqualified, for example "left(right(x) ⟑ right(y))" and "as for geometric_product" (`product.hpp:329-336`). Rule W6 (`authoring-rules.md:130`) qualifies every call with its namespace alias on every page.

Failure: an entry that copies the sentence breaks W6, or reads less well than the rest of the page. An author who adds code font or `core::` breaks check 3. Fix: state in G3 that W6 and code-font conventions do not apply in that field, or that check 3 compares after removing code-span marks and namespace qualifiers, and say which. Does not block.

### Finding 5 (minor, confirmed): G5's "does not hold it yet" goes stale when the parallel branch merges

G5 (`authoring-rules.md:200`) says that `rotation`'s zero direction (`motions.hpp:48-61`) stays in full, "though the checker's list does not hold it yet". On `records/comment-table-rulings`, commit `a8f11b3` adds that place to `KEPT_IN_FULL` (28 places). Whichever branch merges second leaves a sentence in this record that is false. G5 also fixes line ranges in a durable rule, and those ranges change with plan 0048's comment units.

Fix: when the second branch merges, drop "though the checker's list does not hold it yet". Name the places by declaration (`rotation`; the floating case of `operator/` in `linear.hpp`) and not by line. Does not block.

### Finding 6 (minor, confirmed): R5's task can be answered by copying the page's example

R5 (`authoring-rules.md:190`) sets the second cold reader the task of turning (2, −3, 5) by 2 − e12. The pages under test print exactly that case. Section 5.2 asks the first-things fact for "one compiled program on s = 2 − e12 and (2, −3, 5) that asserts the three values of T3", and the card's row shows (6, 17, −25) and (18, −1, 25). A reader can reach (18, −1, 25) by matching the example. That shows the pages state the answer. It does not show that a reader who knows rotors can carry the rule to a new rotor, and R5 calls this read the only test of the `transform` trap's documentation.

Fix: give the reader a rotor and vector the pages do not print, in another plane than e12, with the target derived and checked as T3 was. Pass the pages on that value. Does not block.

### Finding 7 (minor, confirmed): G1's "no heading" for `detail` declarations reads wider than the reference

G1 ends "A declaration in a `detail` namespace has no entry and no heading." The comment table moves the public contract at `mv.hpp:880-882`, which sits in `detail`, to the concepts heading "Scalars the exactness check cannot read". Three more `detail` rows move to concepts and diagnostics headings. Read as written, G1 forbids those headings. Fix: "has no reference entry and no reference heading". Does not block.

### Finding 8 (minor, confirmed): the figure-label rule is an unruled choice with no open item

Notation key rule 9 (`notation-key.md:17`) decides that a figure's labels and alternative text write the two products in words, "until it does", since decision 0258 item 9 names only running text and blocks. That is a reasonable interim choice, but no record lists it as open for the owner or the coordinator. Under rule 9, captions count as running text and use the symbols, so a caption and its figure's alternative text describe one product differently. Fix: list it as an open item, or settle it as a reading in the coordinator's records. Does not block.

### Finding 9 (minor, confirmed): "T1 to T2 again" is wider than the two new files

`misconceptions.md:85` says T3 "and T1 to T2" are checked again by `rulings_values_probe.cpp` and `rulings_values_check.py`. Neither checks the `transform(v, !V)` column of T1 or T2, and the script does not check T1's classical value either. The earlier `transform_sandwich_probe.cpp` and the note's section 2.8 do cover them, and my script checks all fifteen. Fix: "T3, and the first two columns of T1 and T2". Does not block.
