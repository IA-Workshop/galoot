### 2.2 F1: the limit of Clang's step count

Probe `wd/probes/limit.cpp`: `a OP b` for two `mv<alg, full<5>, std::int64_t>` (or `full<5>` with vectors), compiled with `-c`.

| Configuration | conformal(3) `%` |
| --- | --- |
| clang `-O0` | builds |
| clang `-O1`, `-O2`, `-O0 -D_GLIBCXX_NO_ASSERTIONS` | fails |
| clang `-O2 -D_GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS` | builds |
| clang `-O2 -fconstexpr-steps=1200000`, `-O0 -D_GLIBCXX_NO_ASSERTIONS -fconstexpr-steps=1200000` | builds |
| clang `-O2` with 1,050,000 steps / 1,100,000 steps | fails / builds |
| clang `-O0` with 940,000 / 960,000 / 1,000,000 steps | fails / builds / builds, so "about 950,000" holds |
| clang libc++ `-O0`, `-O2` | builds |
| g++ `-O0`, `-O2`, `-O0 -D_GLIBCXX_NO_ASSERTIONS` | builds |
| clang `-O2`: `*`, `^`, `&` on the same operands; `%` of `full<5>` with vectors; `%` in R(5,0,0) and R(4,0,1) | all build |

Dense forms of five generators, `full<5>` × `full<5>`: the two test forms (which have a few zero entries) and a form with every pair coupled (2 on the diagonal, 1 elsewhere). On Clang, `*` and `%` fail in every configuration I tried: `-O0`, `-O2`, `-O2 -D_GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS`, and libc++ at `-O0` and `-O2`. `^` and `&` build. On GCC at `-O0` and `-O2`, `*` and `%` build. So the comments' "in any configuration at its default limits" and "GCC 16 can" hold on three forms. That a dense form never builds is inferred from those three and from `plan.hpp`'s count of 8,319,054 steps for the 2-and-1 form, eight times the limit.

The mechanism, "each array subscript then costs a function call": in libstdc++ 16, `__glibcxx_assert` without `_GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS` calls `std::__is_constant_evaluated()` (`c++config.h`), and with it, it uses `__builtin_expect`. Probe `subscript.cpp` runs 1,000 subscripts of a `std::array` in a `static_assert`. It needs 8,000 to 9,000 steps with the assertions and 12,000 to 14,000 without, so about five more steps per subscript. `c++config.h:607` confirms that the assertions are on by default only without `__OPTIMIZE__`.

### D1. `apply`'s comment still names one exception on Clang, where there are two, and its GCC bound is not qualified by the form. Low; does not block; detail comment.

`include/galoot/core/mv.hpp:780-788`: "The plans build up to full<6> times full<6> (4,096 terms) on GCC 16, and up to full<5> times full<5> on Clang 23, with one exception there: the geometric antiproduct of full<5> in conformal(3)…". Both clauses are false for dense forms:

- On Clang, `*` and `%` of `full<5>` in any dense form of five generators fail at the default limits (section 2.2). `product.hpp`'s header comment, written in the same commit, says so, and so does `product_plan`'s comment ("the form with 2 on the diagonal and 1 elsewhere: … n = 5, 8,319,054 … (over both limits)").
- On GCC, `*` of `full<6>` in the 2-and-1 form of six generators fails: "'constexpr' evaluation operation count exceeds limit of 33554432" (probe `limit6.cpp`). The "(4,096 terms)" implies a diagonal form, but the sentence does not say so.

Failure scenario: a maintainer who reads `apply`'s summary takes `full<5>` × `full<5>` to be safe on Clang for every form except one. Suggested fix: "for diagonal forms" on both bounds, and "in a dense form, see product_plan" for the rest. That is what F1 item 2 asked for.
