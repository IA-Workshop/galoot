# Re-derivation rows: one per convention claim

Part of the [V3 record](README.md). Columns:

- **Item**: the CONVENTIONS 1.18 item, with a letter where one item has several claims. **Label**: its status label in CONVENTIONS.
- **Derivation's result**: what the derivation found, in its own words.
- **Route** and **Tool**: how it was derived; tool codes E (engine), S (sympy 1.14.0), F (python-flint 0.9.0), G (galgebra 0.6.0), C (GCC 16.2.1 and Clang 23.1.5), defined in the README section 3.
- **Rerun**: the script (README section 3 gives the command); every script ends in a `PASS` line.
- **Spec**: agree or disagree with what CONVENTIONS states, or "n/d" where the claim is not derivable.
- **Tests**: agree or disagree with what the tests pin, with the golden namespace and count from `c1`, the hand values from `c2`, and the test files the trace gate lists for the item (`test/core/` unless `rga3/`, which is `test/presets/rga3/`).
- **Not shown**: what the comparison does not establish.

"16 forms" means the Gram matrices of `forms.py`: R(3,0,0), R(2,1,0), R(1,1,1), R(3,0,1), R(2,0,2), R(1,3,0), R(4,1,0), R(4,0,1), conformal(1) to (3), the Witt form of two null pairs, two dense nondegenerate forms (det −11 and 48), and two degenerate non-diagonal forms whose radicals are (1, −1, 1) and (1, 1, 0, −1).
