### MA1 (major, owner action). The drafted errata-naming row rests on wrong facts

- Provisions: C11 and V6 (a section 8 row states the disagreement and a test that tells the alternatives apart); V9 (what a source says); C10 (CONVENTIONS changes only by the owner's ratification, so the owner needs exact, correct text); decisions 0234 item 7 and 0240 item 12.
- Evidence, against the draft in `records/performance/core-unit-w10-index.md`, section "Draft":
  1. **Where the remark is.** The draft puts it "in its remark on the transform of a line". The two records that quote the page say it is a correction to Equation (3.219) of the fifth and earlier printings: "Q_vw should be the coefficient of the scalar term 1, and Q_mw ... of the antiscalar term 𝟙" (`records/reviews/2026-11-10-wu17-part-b-check.md` item 7; `records/spikes/spike-e/README.md` line 83).
  2. **"Read as a slip of naming."** No record says this. The errata entry is itself the author's correction of the book, so the disagreement is between two Lengyel sources: the book as corrected, and the wiki's Motor page. The independent check says exactly this, and that C11 asks for a section 8 row because of it (item 7, "Second"). The draft's reading reverses which side made a correction, and it rests on no evidence.
  3. **"No count or polynomial of the transform depends on which is which."** The check qualifies this. It holds for the counts and for polynomials written in blade coefficients, since h is symmetric in the two letters. It does not hold for formulas written in the letters: the unit constraint λ would read |v|² + Q_mw² under the errata's naming.
  4. **The Test column.** The draft cites "the layout assertions of `test/presets/rga3/objects_test.cpp`, which reject the two orders that would swap them". Neither `!=` assertion swaps the two coefficients (lines 184 to 187). `"e14 e24 e34 e1234 e23 e13 e12 1"` keeps e1234 fourth and 1 eighth, and differs only in orientations. `"1 e41 e42 e43 e23 e31 e12 e1234"` is the grade order. What pins 5.8's placement is the equality `rga3::motor_layout == set_of(motor_names)` (line 177, with `motor_names` at lines 78 to 89: e1234 fourth, 1 eighth) in "the rga3 layouts hold the oriented blades of their names" (line 173). The trace gate lists that test for 5.8 (`objects_test.cpp:173`). The format test is cited correctly.
  5. The index cites "research note 0027, section 1 item 6". The item is number 6 of the note's opening list ("Departures, ambiguities, and open points first", line 17), not section 1, which is "What 'exact' means for form B".
- Corrected text, for the owner to add after the "Conformal duals" row if the row is wanted:

  ```
  | Motor coefficient names | The rigid wiki's Errata page, correcting Equation (3.219) of the fifth and earlier printings of *Projective Geometric Algebra Illuminated*, says that Q_vw is the coefficient of the scalar **1** and Q_mw that of the antiscalar 𝟙.
  ```
