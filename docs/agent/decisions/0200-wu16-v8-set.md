## Context

DoD-17 asks for V8 cases of the rigid model: point groups, chains of joint motions against 4×4 matrices, and incidence. Its reading said "families realizable over ℚ only; hexagonal and trigonal need √3 and are deferred or run under V12". Plan 0023 departure 6 showed that with the three- and sixfold axes along [111] every class has rational versors, up to scale.

## Decision

1. **Amendment of DoD-17's reading.** All 32 crystallographic point classes are V8 cases of rga3, with the fourfold axes of the tetragonal and cubic classes along z and the three- and sixfold axes of the trigonal and hexagonal classes along (1, 1, 1). No family is deferred and none runs under V12; every comparison is exact (V11).
2. **The set as committed** (test group 3, `79abffd`, cherry-picked from `b711850`; reference values in `test/reference/golden.hpp`, written by `golden.py` only where the geometric route of `conventions_check.py` and the linear-algebra route of `rigid_motions.py` agree):
   - Point groups (`rga3.point_groups`, namespace `point_groups`): for each class, the closure of its rational generators under `compose`, at run time over the test rational, through the four antiproducts of motors and flectors; each element normalized by its first nonzero coefficient. Its count equals the order of the published table, and its elements match the 332 golden matrices one to one by their action on (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1), and (1, 2, 3), up to a positive weight.
   - Chains (`rga3.chains`, namespace `joint_chains`): four seeded chains of 3, 4, 5, and 6 joints Rz(θ) Tz(d) Tx(a) Rx(α) with rational half angles; the composed motor is unitized and moves ten points as the golden 4×4 product, exactly.
   - Incidence (`rga3.incidence`, namespaces `rigid_lines`, `rigid_planes`, `rigid_meets`, `rigid_incidence`): eight seeded cases each of line and plane joins, line-plane, plane-plane, and three-plane meets, and point-on-plane, point-on-line, and line-on-line tests, every other one incident by construction; exact zeros where the cases are incident and nonzero values where they are not.
   - Rotations (`rga3.motions`, namespace `rigid_rotations`): 21 rotors with rational half-angle sines and cosines about the axes and about lines off the origin, against right-handed matrices by Rodrigues' formula.
3. **Referents (V8, V9).** The orders against Wikipedia's table of the 32 classes (research note 0023, G2), a named substitute: the International Tables for Crystallography, volume A, could not be read (G1). The chain form against no primary source: Denavit and Hartenberg's paper could not be read (J1); a secondary account gives the same product and closed form (J2), and no claim of conformity to their convention is made. The incidences and rotations rest on linear algebra (cross products, determinants, Rodrigues' formula), a route independent of the library (V1 kind 3).

## Options weighed

- DoD-17's reading as written: the 27 classes realizable with the principal axis along z over ℚ, the trigonal and hexagonal ones deferred or under a rounding tolerance. Fewer exact cases, and a tolerance where none is needed.
- The [111] setting (chosen): every class exact. Its cost is that the trigonal and hexagonal classes are not in the Tables' standard setting (a hexagonal basis with the principal axis along z), so a reader comparing coordinates must conjugate; the orders and the kinds of element do not depend on the setting.

## Consequences

- M4's "rigid V8 cases" are these. What is validated is the orders and the properties G3 lists, against a secondary table; the elements are verified (two independent closures agree), not validated against a primary table.
- If the owner supplies the International Tables or Denavit and Hartenberg's paper (research note 0023, "Not verified, and open", item 1), G2 and J2 can be checked against them with no change to the tests.
