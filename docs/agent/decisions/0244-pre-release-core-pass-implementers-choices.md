## Context

Plan 0040 gathered five small changes before the M7 gate: forced inlining of the kernel chain with a structural test, the deletion of `detail::inverse_change`, a deleted `==` on `basis_change` that names `same_rows`, the two `TODO`s of `mv.hpp`, and the compiler-version limit catalogues moved out of the header comments. The plan fixed what each change does; this record states how each was done where the plan left a choice, where the code made the plan's reading wrong, and what the evidence found that the plan did not expect.

## Decision

### The forced inlining (part A)

1. **One macro, on the thirteen functions of the chain and on the eleven public functions a structure test showed called, and no others.** `GALOOT_ALWAYS_INLINE` in `assert.hpp` is `[[gnu::always_inline]]` under `__GNUC__` or `__clang__` and empty otherwise, with a `///` comment that says what it does, why, that it is not part of the interface, and what a caller compiled for fewer instruction-set features than its unit gets (a compile error; the blind review's m1). With the thirteen alone, the first probe showed no call; the blind review (its M1) then showed that GCC kept the public products out of line where it had inlined them before, and Clang its call at the public function, on motors, flectors and full multivectors, which the first probe did not reach. Applying plan section 1 item 3's rule to a probe that reaches them, the attribute also goes on `geometric_product`, `wedge`, `geometric_antiproduct`, `antiwedge` and their operators `*`, `^`, `%`, `&` (called by both compilers), and on `antidot`, `weight` and `left_weight_dual` (called by GCC from a unit of one function, the latter two only once the products were forced). Each of the eleven is needed on at least one compiler: removing it alone fails a structure test (`records/mutations/0040-pre-release-core-pass/`). No other public function showed a call, in the probe, alone, or in a survey of 61 operations each compiled alone, so the unary operations, `+` and `-`, the scalar products, the other duals, `dot`, `project`, `antiproject`, `change_basis`, `change_basis_back`, the conversion constructor, `truncate` and `part` have no attribute (C12). No `NOLINT` is needed on the macro: clang-tidy's macro check does not flag an attribute.
   - Weighed: bare `[[gnu::always_inline]]` on each function, which repeats a vendor spelling and has no fallback; the attribute on every public operation, which the evidence does not ask for and which widens the reach of the target-mismatch error; `[[gnu::flatten]]` on the public operations, which the advisory report found unreliable on Clang.
2. **The structure test reads the compiler's assembly, not the object, in one unit and one function at a time.** `rga3.kernels_inline` runs `test/tools/kernels_inline.py` on `<compiler> ... -S -o - kernels_inline.cpp`; `rga3.kernels_inline_alone` runs it with `--alone`, which compiles each probe function in a unit of its own (the probe's other paragraphs and that function's), because a compiler weighs a unit's growth against its size and GCC kept `antidot` out of line only when alone. The script fails on a call, a jump or a conditional jump whose operand names a symbol of namespace `galoot` anywhere in it, so that `@PLT` and `*sym@GOTPCREL(%rip)` (a `-fno-plt` build) are caught as well as a direct call; on a call through a register an earlier `lea` or `mov` in the function loaded with such a symbol; on the `H` qualifier of an explicit-object member; on a probe function that the output does not define; and it refuses output that names no 64-bit x86-64 register, so that another target's assembly fails rather than passes (the blind review's m2). The flags are fixed (checking off, `-O2`, `-ffp-contract=off`, the S4 warnings); the include directories and the language standard are read as `galoot_compile_command` reads them, the standard as the last element of its output, which that function documents as last. The probe is also an OBJECT library with each tree's own flags, so that the lint preset sees it; its functions are wrapped in `NOLINTBEGIN/END(misc-use-internal-linkage)`, since they need external linkage to be emitted.
   - Weighed: `nm` on the object, which cannot tell a tail jump from a reference; a fixed list of symbols to forbid, which a new chain function would escape; reading the tree's own flags, which would make the answer depend on the preset (checking on adds calls by design).
3. **The probe: E1's seven functions verbatim and 41 more** (K6, widened after M1): `sum_lines`, `reverse_line`, `dot_points`, `change_point`, which reach `detail::sum` and `run_sum`, the unary `apply` and `run_map`, `run_product` from `dot`, and `run_change`; and 37 that call each public operation by name and by operator on rga3 motors and flectors and on two multivectors of all sixteen blades, with the unary, sum, dual, change-of-basis and conversion wrappers on the latter. The tag of the changes of basis is a change of rigid(3) to itself that doubles e4, defined in the probe, its value `[[maybe_unused]]` for the units that hold one function alone. Fourteen fixture tests, against the plan's six, give the local-entity form, the conditional jump, the register call, the explicit-object form, the refusal of another target, and the three behaviors of `--alone` a test each.
4. **Five attributes the plan held to be guards are needed now.** Removing one attribute at a time, the plan expected `compose`, `transform_middle`, and on GCC `transform` and `run_product` to survive the structure test. They do not: without its attribute, `compose` is called from `compose_motors` on both compilers, `transform` from all three transform probes on GCC, `transform_middle` from `transform_line` on Clang, and, once the public products are forced, `run_product` on GCC too. Forcing a function inline moves its callers' inlining budget, as the advisory report found for `apply`, and the M1 fix is that effect one level further up. Six attributes stay guards (`run_map`, the unary `apply` with a target, `run_sum`, `sum`, `run_change`, `transform_outer`), with `transform_middle` on GCC; the two `apply` overloads with no target layout are reached by no library caller (equivalent). The mutant specs keep the plan's expected verdicts, so `tools/mutate` exits 1 on them, and their README records the observed ones: the record shows the prediction beside the result.
5. **The formatter reads the macro as an attribute** (`c6efd1f`, the merge runner). Without `AttributeMacros: [GALOOT_ALWAYS_INLINE]` in `.clang-format`, clang-format took the macro for the start of a type and indented three of the attributed declarations as continuations. The line only tells the formatter what the token is; no other file's layout changes. It was made outside plan 0040's file table, in its own commit, so that the review can read it alone.
   - Weighed: leaving the formatter's output (three oddly indented declarations); the macro after `[[nodiscard]]`, which formats worse.

### `basis_change` (part B)

6. **The identities test reads the checked inverse**: `core::detail::check_change(change, source, target)`, `REQUIRE`s that its fault is `ChangeFault::none`, and reads `.inverse`. Its references are unchanged (K8).
7. **The deleted `==`**: `bool operator==(const basis_change&) const = delete; // use same_rows(a, b)`, with a `///` comment that says why and what to call. The literal fits the 80 columns with the declaration, so the formatter keeps it on the line that both compilers echo. The compile-fail rows require each compiler's own error text for a deleted function (GCC "use of deleted function", Clang "overload resolution selected deleted operator '=='"), chosen by `CMAKE_CXX_COMPILER_ID`, and the literal `// use same_rows(a, b)`. This supersedes the last sentence of decision 0235 item 1, which declared no `==` because `= delete("…")` needs C++26: a deleted declaration with a comment needs no message argument (decision 0236 item 3).
8. **P8 for part B was the whole suite**, not only the plan's `^core\.basis_change` selection, because the rga3 preset includes `core.hpp`, which includes `basis_change.hpp`.

### The build cost (W10) and O2

13. **The campaign uses the measure trees for "release"** (plan section 1 item 6, K7), and its seven units and the knob criterion are those plan 0040 section 9 fixed before any run. It ran twice: at `7a27f76` (`records/performance/pre-release-core-pass/cost/`) and, after the M1 fix changed the chain, at `a6f3196` (`cost-public-ops/`). **The criterion is met both times.** At `a6f3196` six debug units grow by more than twice the larger range of their repeats and by more than 5%: on GCC `product_test.cpp` +80.5%, `transform_test.cpp` +35.8%, `chains_test.cpp` +17.4% and E1's probe +8.4%; on Clang `product_test.cpp` +17.5% and `chains_test.cpp` +5.0%. GCC's debug compile of `product_test.cpp` takes 4.32 s against 2.18 s and 830 MiB against 418 MiB. As the plan says, no knob is added: the numbers go to the owner as O2, whose recorded default, (a) no knob, stands until the owner rules.

## Consequences

- Every operation the probe reaches compiles to the arithmetic of its terms at its call site on both compilers at `-O2`, with the structure test's flags (checking off, no contraction, x86-64), both inside the probe's one unit and alone in a unit of its own: the 48 functions of `test/bench/kernels_inline.cpp`, which call each public product and its operator on rga3 motors and flectors and on two full multivectors, and the unary, sum, dual, change-of-basis and conversion wrappers, and 61 operations of a survey, each compiled alone. A call into the library that comes back in one of the 48 fails `rga3.kernels_inline` or `rga3.kernels_inline_alone` on the compiler that makes it. Not shown, so not claimed: other layouts, other algebras, scalar types other than `double`, other optimization levels (at `-Os` the blind review found calls on both compilers, at `721afe7`), and checking builds, where `transform` calls its checks out of line by design.
- A caller compiled for fewer instruction-set features than its unit cannot call a forced operation (m1); the macro's comment says so and how to avoid it. Forcing the public products makes this reachable from more call sites.
- At `-O0` each call of a forced function carries its body: E1's probe's text grows 12.0% on GCC and 1.8% on Clang, against the branch point; at `-O2` it shrinks (GCC -0.4%, Clang -8.8%).
- `a == b` and `a != b` on two `basis_change` values fail to compile with one error whose note names `same_rows`.
- The header comments no longer state compiler-version sizes; until the documentation lane's `docs/guide/limits.md` is on `develop`, their pointers name a heading of a page not yet there (plan 0040 risk 6, K10).
